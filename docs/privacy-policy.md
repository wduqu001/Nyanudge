# NyaNudge privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · App: NyaNudge (`com.quoryn.nyanudge.app`)

NyaNudge is a reminder app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your data anywhere.

## What the app stores
Your reminders, settings and the history of reminders you marked done are kept only on your device. We cannot see them.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** show your reminders.
- **Alarms and reminders (exact alarms)** make reminders arrive on time.
- **Run at startup** restores scheduled reminders after the device restarts.

Reminders are scheduled on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

## Crash logs
If you turn on crash logs, error details are saved on your device. They leave the device only if you export and share them yourself.

## Sharing
We do not sell, share or transfer your data. The app contains no third-party tracking or advertising code.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings › Clear all history** to erase your history. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

## Not medical advice
NyaNudge is not a medical device. Reminders can be late or missed, for example if notifications are turned off, so do not rely on it alone for medication.

## Children
The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
